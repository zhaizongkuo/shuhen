Shuhen — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-05 · 中文

Shuhen collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.
No account, no backend, no analytics.

Where your data lives

Your highlights, notes, colours, and the title and URL of each highlighted page are stored in your browser's chrome.storage.local — on this device only.

This is local, not sync. sync would replicate your data to your other devices through the browser vendor's servers; local does not. Your highlights never reach the browser vendor either.

Uninstalling the extension deletes this data along with it.

What we never do

  • Send data to any server — there is not a single network request anywhere in the code
  • Use any third-party analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDK
  • Read or upload your browsing history
  • Ask you to register, sign in, or provide an email address

Permissions

At install time, Shuhen requests access to no websites at all.

PermissionWhy
storageKeep your highlights on this device
activeTabWhen you click the toolbar icon, read the current page's address so we can ask you to authorise that one site
scriptingInject the highlighting code into sites you have authorised
Site accessGranted per site. Shuhen works on a site only after you click the icon there and approve it

You can revoke any site at any time from chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions).

What we read on pages you authorise

So that a highlight can return to the right place after a reload, a site redesign, or infinite scrolling, Shuhen reads the text content of the page, and records the passage you selected plus 32 characters of context on either side.

This is used only in your browser's memory and local storage. It never leaves your device.

Export

Exporting Markdown or plain text writes to your clipboard or downloads a file to your own computer. No server is involved.

Children's privacy

Shuhen collects no data, and therefore collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated. If any form of data upload is ever introduced, it will be disclosed in the extension and require your consent.
